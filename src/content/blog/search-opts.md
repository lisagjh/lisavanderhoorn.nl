The first person ever that didn't come from my LinkedIn or other social media accounts, but from Claude. Last week, I noticed someone who came from Brave Search. But now I'm curious about how it works, and why this one post shows up. Most likely the only reason this scroll-state post is showing up because it's a less written about topic.

## How Does SEO Work?

SEO works by optimizing a website to improve its visibility in search engine results. Search engines crawl to internet to index pages, and use algorithms with all kinds of factors to determine which results best match a search query. SEO focuses on three main areas: content, links, and technical SEO.

Content, also called "on-page SEO" means it's relevant and helpful information. Links means there's other websites linking to this website. Technical SEO means a site if fast, secure, and user-friendly.

So for the content or on-page SEO it looks at relevant keywords in the page, mostly in titles, headings and body text.
